# 🐄 PashuRaksha AI

### AI-Assisted Livestock Health Early-Warning & Outbreak Surveillance Platform

> **Smart India Hackathon 2026 — SIH26128**

PashuRaksha AI is an AI-assisted livestock health surveillance and decision-support platform designed to support **early reporting, risk indication, veterinary verification, disease surveillance, prevention, and coordinated livestock health management**.

The platform connects farmers, veterinary professionals, and laboratory workflows through a unified digital system with support for **GIS surveillance, environmental context, offline reporting, multilingual interfaces, and AI-assisted risk analysis**.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

**SIH26128 — Efficient systems for early detection, prevention, and management of livestock diseases and animal health issues**

Livestock disease management in rural areas can be affected by:

- Delayed reporting of symptoms and animal deaths
- Limited access to veterinary services
- Fragmented animal health records
- Lack of real-time regional disease visibility
- Difficulties in identifying emerging disease patterns
- Poor connectivity in rural areas
- Incomplete vaccination and treatment histories
- Delayed coordination between farmers, veterinarians, and laboratories

These challenges can delay intervention and increase the risk of livestock mortality, productivity loss, and disease spread.
